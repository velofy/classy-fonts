Every face here is free. Commercial-use fonts render live and download as real files; personal-use fonts are shown as specimens linking to their designer.

What “free” means here

Free to use and free to redistribute are different rights, so this cabinet keeps them separate rather than dumping every file into one folder.

OFL / Apache: vendored in this repo as real .woff2 + .ttf. Yours to download, use, and redistribute.
Fontshare: free for personal & commercial use; served live from the foundry's own CDN (their licence permits it), download from source.
Personal use: free for personal projects; shown as specimens, download from the designer.
